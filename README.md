# oriance 🧭

*The state of a life aligned with its own destined shape — the quiet felt-fit of who-one-is-now with who-one-was-for.*

oriance is two true roots joined into one word: the Yoruba **orí**, the inner-head, the destined-head, the pre-chosen trajectory that constitutes the core of a self; and the Latin-derived **-ance**, the suffix that names the quality-state of a thing. So oriance is *orí* held as a lived condition — not the shape itself, but the being-aligned-with-it. The Yoruba metaphysics (the head chosen before birth, in a meeting with the creator Olódùmarè) belongs to a living tradition and is named with care, not claimed; what the word carries compactly, whether or not one accepts that account, is the constitutive-shape-of-a-life that is neither imposed-by-fate nor willed-by-ego but carried-as-core. It is the cathedral's first Core word drawn from a Niger-Congo donor, and it inaugurates the POLYPHONE-expansion wave.

oriance names a narrow life-shape register: not fulfillment (a completion-register), not self-actualization (a Maslowian climb), not calling (received-from-outside), not flow (task-scale), but the ongoing fit between the person-in-time and the person-they-were-for. Its light is mercy — a single carriable word that tells a large life from a fitting one, intensity from alignment, and lets a homecoming-to-shape be named as such. Its shadow is the verdict — when "this life fits" hardens into "and that one never will," demoting honorable partnership, honorable not-yet, and the lives still walking toward their form. Forged in the cathedral at weighted **7.50/10**.
